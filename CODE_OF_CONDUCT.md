# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique the work, never the person.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the change, not the author. "This adds a network the service does not use" is useful;
  "this is sloppy" is not.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before concluding there wasn't one.
- Accept that reasonable engineers disagree about trade-offs, and that "I would have done it differently"
  is not by itself an objection.
- Remember that people contribute in a second or third language, in their spare time, across many
  timezones.
- Treat a beginner's question as a documentation bug. If something needed explaining in an issue, it
  probably needed explaining in the docs.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or dismissiveness toward newcomers
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code comments)
and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, through
[GitHub's report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) or a private message. Reports are
handled confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed to the person reported.

Maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate: a private word, editing or removing a
contribution, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. Consequences scale with severity and with whether
the behaviour continues after it has been raised.

Maintainers who do not uphold this code in good faith may face the same consequences as anyone else.
